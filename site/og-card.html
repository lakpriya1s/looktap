looktap

Hands on the phone.

Your coding agent reads any iPhone or Android device as text, and taps by label.

npm install -g @looktap/cli · 0 dependencies · iPhone and Android
$ looktap ls
@1  Heading     24,104 140x41  Sign in
@4  TextField   24,236 342x48  Email
@8  Button      24,432 342x52  Sign in
$ looktap tap "Sign in" --ls
tapped "Sign in" at 195,458
@1  Heading     20,100 190x41  Demo Store
